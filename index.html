How ProudMe works

A simple rhythm kids can make their own.

Healthy routines stick when the next step feels clear, achievable, and worth celebrating. ProudMe keeps that daily loop light.

  1. 1
    🏃🥗🌙📵
    Choose

    Start with one small goal.

    Kids pick an age-appropriate focus across activity, eating, sleep, or screen time. No overwhelming lists—just a next step that fits today.

  2. 2
    LearnTry it
    Practice

    Learn, play, and reflect.

    Short lessons, educational games, and friendly Pebble check-ins help kids connect a healthy choice with how their day actually feels.

  3. 3
    ✓
    Celebrate

    Let progress feel visible.

    Streaks, achievements, and encouraging reflections turn repeated effort into momentum—without making a missed day feel like failure.

Meet Pebble
Encouragement with boundaries

A kid-safe buddy, with grown-ups still in the loop.

Pebble helps children reflect on healthy habits. It does not replace a parent, teacher, counselor, doctor, or emergency service.

Focused conversations
Pebble stays centered on age-appropriate wellness and everyday reflection.
Layered safeguards
Messages and proposed responses are checked before a reply reaches a child.
Short retention
Chats automatically delete after 30 days and are not used to build advertising profiles.
Clear human path
Serious concerns direct children toward a trusted adult and appropriate crisis support.
