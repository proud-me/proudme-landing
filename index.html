Meet your buddy

Hi, I'm Pebble.

A kid-safe AI buddy your child can actually talk to. Built for kids 7-11, reviewed by the LSU lab, and designed to keep the hard moments safe.

Pebble is your kid's quiet check-in friend. They remind you to log your journal, nudge you toward small healthy habits (one more glass of water, a stretch break, an earlier bedtime), and are there to chat when you need to talk something through.

  • 💬 Mood-first check-ins. Pebble asks how you feel before asking what you did.
  • 🛡️ Crisis-safe replies. Any sign of distress routes to 988 and Crisis Text Line, every time.
  • ⏱️ Auto-deletes in 30 days. No long-term chat history. No training on your child's words.

Security

Hardened for kids. Reviewed continuously.

Children's data needs more than a checkbox. Here is what ships behind the scenes, and how we keep proving it.

Last reviewed 2026-05-16
🔐

Encrypted everywhere

HTTPS-only API. Bcrypt cost-12 password hashing. JWT with server-side revocation. iOS Keychain and Android EncryptedSharedPreferences for on-device secrets.

🛡️

Fail-closed AI safety

Every message from Pebble runs through OpenAI's safety classifier. If the classifier is unreachable, we block the message instead of passing it through. Crisis terms route to 988 and Crisis Text Line.

🧪

Ongoing pen testing

Every shipped change is reviewed by an experienced security reviewer before merge. Most recent pass: 8 findings (2 HIGH and 6 MEDIUM) fixed and verified on 2026-05-16.

📋

Audit trail by design

Every safety event (crisis response, harmful-input redirect, moderation outage) is written to an immutable audit log. IRB-reviewable. Exportable on request.

🔍

Responsible disclosure

Found something? Email pklab@lsu.edu. We acknowledge within 48 hours and credit security researchers in release notes.

⚡

Fast patch cycle

Security fixes go from finding to shipped in hours, not weeks. Every release runs the full automated test suite plus a security review pass before it hits production.

Privacy

Built around the parent, not the advertiser.

No ads, no profiles, no data sold. The least amount of data we need, kept the shortest time it is useful for.

👨‍👩‍👧

Verifiable parental consent

Required before any account is created. COPPA-compliant flow designed with the LSU Office of General Counsel. Parents control the account, not the child.

🎓

IRB oversight

LSU Institutional Review Board approved on 2025-02-05 under protocol IRBAM-25-0015. Operated directly by the LSU Pedagogical Kinesiology Lab as a lab-based research project; the App is not run through any child's school.

⏱️

Your buddy, Pebble's, chats auto-delete

Conversations with the AI buddy are kept for 30 days, then automatically and irreversibly deleted. No long-term chat history, no training on your child's words.

🚫

Zero third-party trackers

No analytics SDKs. No ad networks. No behavioral profiling. The marketing site itself has zero trackers. Verified at every release.

🧹

Data minimization

We ask for the least we need to make the app work. No phone numbers, no location, no contacts, no photos. Just what each feature genuinely requires.

📤

Parent rights

View, export, or delete your child's data at any time. Email pklab@lsu.edu and we respond within 7 days, usually same-day.
